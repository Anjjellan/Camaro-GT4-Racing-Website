Selected Car: Chevrolet Camaro GT4.R

Development and Purpose

The Chevrolet Camaro GT4.R was developed by Pratt Miller in 2017 specifically for GT4 international racing 5.

Key points about its development:

  • Based on the Camaro ZL1 road car
  • Regulations prevented using the ZL1's supercharged engine, so they used the naturally aspirated LT1 6.2L V8 from the Camaro SS instead
  • Engine modifications included direct fuel injection, carbon fiber intake, custom camshaft, data acquisition system, and ECU

Technical Specifications

Some key specifications of the GT4.R include:

  • Engine: Naturally aspirated 6.2L V8 (480 hp)
  • Transmission: 6-speed sequential paddle-shift
  • Brakes: Brembo 6-piston front, 4-piston rear
  • Suspension: Adjustable anti-roll bars, Öhlins dampers
  • Weight: 3,131 lbs
  • Wheels/Tires: 18-inch forged alloys with racing slicks

Racing History

The GT4.R made its debut at the Pirelli World Challenge race in St. Petersburg, Fla., and was campaigned by the Illinois-based Blackdog Speed Shop with drivers Lawson Aschenbach and Tony Gaples in 2017.

The Camaro GT4.R made its debut at the Continental Tire SportsCar Challenge in the Grand Sport (GS) Class at Circuit of the Americas in Austin, Texas, in 2017.

In 2023, V8 Racing made a strong return to the GT4 European Series, fielding the Chevrolet Camaro GT4.R for its final season. Drivers Dante Rappange from the Netherlands and Belgian teammate Kenny Herremans, who previously achieved an overall victory in the last race of 2022 in Barcelona and finished as Pro-Am runners-up, stepped up to the Silver class together.
